Privacy Policy

Version: 2026-10-01 · Türkçe sürüm. If the two versions differ, the Turkish version prevails.

This policy explains what personal data the Pazarlıkçı Android app (the "App") collects, why, who it is shared with, and your rights. The App is developed by Nurullah Cirit ("we"), who is the data controller. Contact: nma.cirit@gmail.com.

1. Data we collect

  • Account and contact: name, e-mail address, mobile phone number verified by SMS, optional profile photo and bio. If you sign in with Google, your Google account name and e-mail address.
  • Location: a negotiator's saved service location (to find nearby posts); the meeting point a buyer enters on a post; a one-time precise location taken when you tap "Geldim" (I've arrived) on the meeting day. The App does not track location in the background.
  • In-app content and activity: posts, applications, messages between users, selections, meeting and arrival records, mutual ratings and reviews, reports and blocked users.
  • Subscription: the Google Play status and purchase token of the negotiator subscription. Payments are handled by Google Play; we never receive your card details.
  • Device and usage: notification token, app instance ID, app version, device model, usage statistics and crash/error logs.

2. How we use it

  • To create your account and verify sign-in and your phone number.
  • To match buyers with negotiators and show negotiators nearby posts.
  • To provide messaging, and to share phone numbers between the two parties after a negotiator is selected so they can call each other.
  • To confirm meeting attendance and compute reliability, ratings and other profile metrics.
  • To send notifications (new applications, selections, messages, reminders, etc.).
  • To verify and manage subscriptions.
  • To keep the service safe, prevent abuse and fraud, and review reports.
  • To measure stability, fix bugs and improve the service.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising, and the App contains no ads.

3. What other users see

  • Public profile (signed-in users): name, profile photo, bio, categories, province/district, rating and other metrics, and reviews once revealed.
  • Posts: negotiators see only an approximate area (district level). The exact meeting point is shown only to the selected negotiator.
  • A negotiator's exact saved location is never shown to other users.
  • Phone numbers are shared only between the two parties of a meeting, after a negotiator is selected.
  • Messages are visible only to the participants of the conversation.

4. Service providers and transfers

The App uses the following Google LLC services. Data is stored and processed on Google servers in Europe (europe-west1, Belgium), which is a transfer outside Türkiye.

  • Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions: accounts, data and file storage, server processing.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: notifications.
  • Firebase App Check and Play Integrity: verifying the App is an unmodified copy.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics: usage statistics and crash reports.
  • Google Play Billing: subscription payments.
  • Google Maps: map display and location picking.

How Google processes data: policies.google.com/privacy. Apart from this, data may be disclosed to authorities only when required by law.

5. Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account is open. You can delete your account in the App (Profil > Hesabı sil) or by e-mail request. Details: Account and data deletion. Analytics and crash data are kept by Google under its own retention periods (at most 14 months).

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access is restricted by server-side authentication and authorization rules.

7. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

8. Your rights

Under Turkish data protection law (KVKK, Law No. 6698, Art. 11) you can request access to, correction or deletion of your data, and object to processing. Send requests to nma.cirit@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always published on this page, and significant changes are announced in the App.
